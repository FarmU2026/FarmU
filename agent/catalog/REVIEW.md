# Review sheet for the agronomist (catalog 2026-10-16.1)

Generated by `agent/catalog/review_sheet.py` from `conditions.json`, `loss_tables.json`, the scorer and the evidence engine. Do not edit by hand; rerun the script. Every number is a DRAFT. Source of the design: `docs/research/grounding.md`. Weight = log-likelihood ratio: +w on `supports`, -w on `refutes` (1.0 = odds x2.7, 2.0 = x7.4, 3.0 = x20).

## 0. How FieldWatch decides, in five steps

1. **Code evaluates every evidence key, every run** (`fieldwatch/evidence.py`): `present`, `absent` or `not_evaluable` (the data is missing). No language model chooses a key. A model may only read a photo or the farmer's words into the fixed photo and farmer keys below.
2. **Each key has a class.** `field` = from this field's own pixels, geometry, event overlap, or an observation of this field (photo, farmer). `static` = local context that does not change with the season (soil, terrain, flood zone, crop history). `regional` = weather, drought category, county disease reports.
3. **Caps.** Regional evidence in total moves a condition by at most +-0.7 log-odds, static +-1.5, field +-4. Items in a correlated **cluster** (rain, low ground, drainage, flood zone ...) count once: the strongest, per direction.
4. **Naming rule.** A condition is named only if it is inside its **stage window**, nothing **rules it out**, at least one **required field-evidence** key is present, and its probability is at least 65% and 15% ahead of the runner-up. Some conditions are capped at 'possible' until the farmer or a photo confirms.
5. **Otherwise: not determined.** The farmer is told what was ruled out, what is possible, and is offered a scouting pin. FieldWatch never guesses a cause. Farmer text uses the tiers likely / possible / not determined, never percentages (the probabilities are not calibrated yet).

The catalog has 70 (condition, key) pairs over 62 distinct keys; 16 are photo or farmer inputs (read by a model into fixed fields), the rest are computed in code. Dead keys (in the catalog, no resolver): none. Orphan resolvers: none.

### What changed from catalog 2026-10-10.2

- Evidence keys now carry `direction`, `evidence_class`, optional `cluster`, and `how` (how code decides). The scorer ignores an evidence item whose direction contradicts the catalog.
- **Removed bonuses (priors kept):** nitrogen corn +0.5 and its stage terms (+0.3, +0.3, -1.0): no source, and they alone made nitrogen 'likely' on rain (memo 5.2). All other `stage_adjust` terms are replaced by the hard stage windows (memo 5.3). Crop and irrigation terms stay.
- **Removed keys:** `nitrogen.pattern_uniform_pale` (uniform paleness is not nitrogen-specific, memo 3.2), `nitrogen.ndre_low_ndvi_normal` (replaced by the sufficiency-index keys `n_red_edge_si_low` and `n_red_edge_si_borderline`), `unknown.no_pattern_found` and `unknown.clear_pattern_found` (not-determined is now an outcome, not a competing hypothesis).
- **Direction corrections:** `pivot_failure.stress_uniform_field` now *refutes* (the old code counted it as support while the reason said the opposite); `nitrogen.prior_crop_soybeans` now *refutes* (rotation credit lowers the need; the old run counted it as support).
- **New required evidence per condition**, stage windows, rule-outs, tier caps and scouting checklists (below).
